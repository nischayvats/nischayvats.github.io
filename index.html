Business AnalysisLive dashboard

Olist E-Commerce: Customer Retention & Delivery Performance

A business review of a Brazilian marketplace: 99,441 orders across 8 tables (1.5M+ rows). I built the MySQL database, wrote 20 SQL queries, segmented customers with RFM, and turned the findings into three recommendations with KPIs.

  • 97% of 93,358 customers never ordered again, so retention is the core problem, not acquisition.
  • Late orders average 2.6★ vs 4.3★ on time; 54% of late orders get a 1–2★ review.
  • RFM found 21,888 high-value At Risk customers holding R$5.0M of past spend.
  • Bahia and Rio have the worst late-delivery rates (14.0%, 13.5%) of the 10 largest states.

Retinal Disease & Glaucoma Detection with ResNet50 and Grad-CAM

Research accepted at ICDSBS 2026 (Scopus-indexed). A fine-tuned ResNet50 classifies eight retinal conditions from fundus images, and Grad-CAM heatmaps show which part of the eye drove each prediction, so doctors can check the model's reasoning.

  • Fine-tuned ResNet50 with transfer learning on the RFMiD fundus image dataset.
  • Compared model variants with ablation studies and comparison tables to justify the final design.
  • Grad-CAM heatmaps make predictions explainable to non-technical reviewers.
  • Co-authored with Aasin Sayyad, supervised by Dr. R. Yamini, SRMIST.
